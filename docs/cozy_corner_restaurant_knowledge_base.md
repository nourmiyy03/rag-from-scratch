## Restaurant Overview

The Cozy Corner is a family-friendly restaurant located at 24 Garden
Street, Brookfield. It opened in March 2021 and serves breakfast, lunch,
and dinner. The restaurant focuses on homemade comfort food made with
locally sourced ingredients whenever possible.

The restaurant has 42 indoor seats and 16 outdoor patio seats. It has a
relaxed atmosphere, free Wi-Fi, a wheelchair-accessible entrance, and
high chairs for young children.

The restaurant does not currently have branches in other cities.

## Events and Private Dining

The Cozy Corner has a small private dining room that accommodates up to
12 guests. It can be reserved for birthdays, family gatherings, and
small business meetings.

Private room requests must be made at least seven days in advance. A
minimum food-and-drink spend of \$250 applies to private room bookings.
A \$50 deposit is required to confirm the reservation and is deducted
from the final bill.

The restaurant does not provide live music or a dedicated event planner.
Customers may bring simple decorations, provided they do not damage the
room or obstruct walkways.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the restaurant open on Mondays?

No. The Cozy Corner is closed every Monday.

### Does the restaurant have vegetarian food?

Yes. Several dishes are vegetarian or can be adapted. Customers should
confirm ingredients with staff.

### Is there a vegan menu?

There is no separate vegan menu. A few dishes can be adapted, but
customers should ask staff about ingredients and preparation.

### Can I reserve a table for 10 people online?

No. Online reservations are limited to 8 guests. Groups of 9 to 16 must
call the restaurant.

### Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available to all customers. Ask a staff member for
the password.

### Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The main entrance is wheelchair accessible. The private dining room
is on the ground floor.

### Does the restaurant offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available within 5 kilometers through QuickPlate, with
a \$15 minimum order.

### Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are allowed on the outdoor patio but are not permitted inside,
except for service animals.

### Does the restaurant accept cash?

Yes. It accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, and contactless payments.

### Can I bring my own birthday cake?

Yes, with advance notice. A \$10 cake service fee applies. The fee is
waived for private room bookings.
